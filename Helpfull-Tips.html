Helpfull Tips

Develop Minor Pieces First

  • Knights

    • Knights on the edge of the board are bad.

  • Bishops

    • Don't move to an easily targetted square.

Control the Center of the Board

  • King and Queen Pawns

    • Attack the central squares.

  • Bishop Pawns

    • Also use to attack central pawns to gain space.

    Move to protect your pawn structure.

Protect Your King

  • Castle

    • Protects king with rook.

    Solid pawn structure to protect.

  • Don't Move King Pawns

    • Weakens pawn structure.

    Liability to checkmates.

Gain Space

  • Move and Attack With Pawns

    • Oposite of castled king pawns are great for attacking.

    Create pawn storms to attack oposing king.

Look for Tempos

  • Don't Just Develop

    • Attack and threaten while developing pieces.

    Develop with a purpose.

Don't Move King-Side Pawns Early

  • Weakens Pawn Structure
  • Gives More Checkmate Oportunities For the Oponent

Bring Every Piece Into an Attack

  • Makes Easier Attacks
  • Checkmate Is More Guarenteed
