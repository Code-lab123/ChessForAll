French Defense

The French Defense is a resilient, counter-attacking opening for Black against White's classic 1.e4. By starting with 1...e6, Black prepares to disrupt the center on the very next move with d7-d5. This establishes a solid, pyramid like pawn chain where Black accepts a slightly cramped position and a temporarily locked-in light-squared bishop in exchange for an hard to break defensive wall and counter-play against White's pawn base.

The Core Move Sequence


  1. e4 e6 (The defining French setup, preparing d7-d5)
  2. d4 d5 (White claims the full center; Black challenges it immediately)
  3. e5 c5 (The Advance Variation baseline; White locks the center, Black strikes the d4 base)
  4. c3 Nc6 (White reinforces the center pyramid; Black brings out the queenside knight)
  5. Nf3 Bd7 (White develops to guard d4; Black readies the light-squared bishop for duty)

White's Objectives

Control Spacial Advantage: White uses the advanced e5-pawn to slow and attack Black's kingside to restrict the natural development of Black's pieces.
Kingside Breakthrough: White uses the extra space to safely manuver minor pieces toward Black's kingside, setting up sacrifices on h7 or f6.

Black's Objectives

Undermining the Base: Black uses intense, and continuous pressure on White's d4-pawn anchor using the c7-c5 break, a fast queenside knight development, and the move Qb6.
The F6 Liberation: Black aims to destroy White's central wedge by timing a f7-f6 pawn strike to open lines for rooks and activate minor pieces.
