French Defense

The French Defense is a highly resilient, counter-attacking opening for Black against White's 1.e4. By playing an immediate 1...e6, Black prepares to dispute the center on the very next move with d7-d5. This establishes a rock-solid, asymmetrical pawn chain where Black gladly accepts a slightly cramped position and a temporarily locked-in light-squared bishop in exchange for an unbreakable defensive wall and explosive counter-play against White's pawn base.

The Core Move Sequence


  1. e4 e6 (The defining French setup, preparing d7-d5)
  2. d4 d5 (White claims the full center; Black challenges it immediately)
  3. e5 c5 (The Advance Variation baseline; White locks the center, Black strikes the d4 base)
  4. c3 Nc6 (White reinforces the center pyramid; Black brings out the queenside knight)
  5. Nf3 Bd7 (White develops to guard d4; Black readies the light-squared bishop for duty)

White's Objectives

The Spatial Clamp: White uses the advanced e5-pawn to permanently cramp Black's kingside and restrict the natural development of Black's pieces.
Kingside Breakthrough: White leverages the extra space to safely steer minor pieces toward Black's kingside camp, setting up powerful sacrifices on h7 or f6.

Black's Objectives

Undermining the Base: Black exerts intense, continuous pressure on White's d4-pawn anchor using the c7-c5 break, a fast queenside knight development, and Qb6.
The F6 Liberation: Black aims to completely shatter White's central wedge by timing a sharp f7-f6 pawn strike to open lines for rooks and activate the pieces.
