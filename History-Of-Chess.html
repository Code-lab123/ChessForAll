History of Chess

The game of chess began in 6th-century India as an ancient war game named chaturanga.From India, it traveled to Persia where players changed its name to shatranj. Arab traders then brought the game across the Middle East and North Africa. By the medieval era, European royalty fell in love with the game and made it a status symbol. Southern Europeans overhauled the rules during the 15th century to speed up the pace of play. This change transformed the weak advisor into the incredibly powerful queen that we know today. Public coffeehouses later became the hotbeds for regular players to study opening strategies and tactics. Today, millions of people worldwide still play this exact version on wooden boards and digital screens alike.
