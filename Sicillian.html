Sicillian

The Sicilian Defense is the most popular and highest-scoring response to White’s 1.e4. Instead of symmetrically copying White with 1...e5, Black fights for the center from the very first move using the c-pawn. This creates an asymmetrical position where Black denies White a peaceful game, playing aggressively for a win right from the opening.

The Core Move Sequence


  1. e4 c5 (The defining Sicilian counter-punch)
  2. Nf3 d6 (Preparing to fight for the d4 square)
  3. d4 cxd4 (The central pawn trade)
  4. Nxd4 Nf6 (Black develops with a direct threat on e4)
  5. Nc3 (White defends, completing the Open Sicilian baseline)

White's Objectives

King-Side Attack: White usually castles kingside (or queenside in sharp variations) and launches a direct pawn storm against Black's king.
Knight Outposts: White aims to plant a powerful knight on the d5 or f5 squares to paralyze Black's position.

Black's Objectives

The c-File Expressway: Black gains an open c-file. Rooks should immediately move to c8 to pressure White's queenside.
The d5 Break: Black’s ultimate goal is to safely push d6–d5. If Black pulls this off cleanly, they usually equalize or take the advantage.
